      Rate requirements for your target tier

      Starts with the 4.85 rating and 4% cancellation limits from Uber's help pages. These vary by city and tier and changed in 2026, so match them to your app. Leave a box empty if it doesn't apply.

      Where the numbers come from. Uber doesn't let outside apps read Uber Pro points, so this planner works from what you type in. Open Uber Pro in the Driver app for your points, the date the period ends and what each tier needs. Your rating and rates are on the same screens.

      How the plan works. Points needed are split across the days left, counting today, at the number of days you drive each week. Your pace is your points divided by the days so far this period. Points reset to zero when a new period starts.
