      Where the numbers come from. Uber doesn't let outside apps read Uber Pro, so this planner works from what you type in. Everything it asks for is on How Uber Pro works in the Driver app.

      How the plan works. Points needed are split across the days left, counting today, at the number of days you drive each week. Trips use your own mix of 1-point and 3-point trips. Your pace is your points divided by the days so far this period. Status is the highest tier where you meet every requirement, and points reset to zero when a new period starts.
